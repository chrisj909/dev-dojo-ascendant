> POST-MVP. Tuning constants live in `IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC.md`, not here.

---

## 1. CONCEPT

You are a **headmaster**, not a fighter. You recruit students, put them through
curricula, and raise them to mastery. When a student masters your teaching they
**graduate and found a branch dojo** under your school — and your power grows
not by accumulating more fighters, but by accumulating **generations**.

Browser-based, asynchronous, server-authoritative. No twitch input. Sessions are
two minutes or fifteen, never forty.

### 1.1 Core pillars

1. **Transmission over accumulation.** Progress is measured in students raised to
   mastery, not in headcount or currency.
2. **A small, legible roster.** 8–12 active students. Every one of them is
   someone you made decisions about.
3. **A style that emerges.** You never pick a fighting style. You teach, and the
   style crystallizes out of what you taught.
4. **One real decision per challenge.** Combat resolves automatically, but who
   you send against a scouted composition is genuine judgment.
5. **Neglect has teeth.** Loyalty and injury decay constantly. A lineage you stop
   tending defects.

### 1.2 What this is not

Not a fighting game. Not a clicker. Not an idle game with a martial arts skin.
The player never controls a fighter, never taps for damage, and never watches a
number tick up without having chosen what feeds it.

### 1.3 Platform

Web. Desktop and mobile browser, responsive, single codebase. Design for the
mobile check-in first — the two-minute session is the atom of retention.

## 2. THE CORE LOOP

### 2.1 Session shapes

| Session | Duration | Player does |
|---|---|---|
| **Check-in** | ~2 min | Collect tuition, assign drills, spend Focus if any banked |
| **Sit-down** | ~15 min | Scout, challenge, restructure curricula, promote, graduate, reassign patronage |
| **Weekly** | ~30 min | Tournaments, region standings settle, lineage review |

### 2.2 The loop in one line

Energy builds students → students become graduates → graduates become branches →
branches generate tribute, territory and the next generation → which raises your
tier → which unlocks deeper regions and more patronage.

Meanwhile Focus spends students against rivals, and injury/loyalty erode them.
**Every system either feeds the roster or threatens it.**

---

## 3. THE HEADMASTER

Your character exists but never fights. Attributes are **teaching multipliers**.

| Attribute | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Technique** | Drill efficiency — XP returned per Energy spent |
| **Mental** | Loyalty decay resistance; poaching defence |
| **Presence** | Recruit pool quality; challenge invitations; branch tribute rate |
| **Stamina** | Energy cap and regen; Focus cap |
| **Discipline** | Injury rate reduction across the roster |

**Nationality** sets your starting lineage (which scroll family you begin with)
and grants one permanent bonus. It does **not** lock your style.

Headmaster attributes rise slowly, from graduations rather than from drills —
you learn by teaching. This keeps the player's own progression tied to the
central verb instead of running parallel to it.

## 4. STUDENTS

### 4.1 Roster cap

**8 at Backyard, rising to 12 at International.** Hard cap. This is a dojo, not a
barracks. The cap is what makes each student legible and each graduation
meaningful — you feel the empty mat.

### 4.2 Student properties

- **Attributes**: Strength, Speed, Technique, Stamina, Mental
- **Aptitude**: per-category learning rate (striking / grappling / internal / weapons)
- **Temperament**: modifies loyalty decay, injury risk, drill preference
- **Belt**: progression track, gates which curricula they can enter
- **Loyalty**: 0–100, decays passively
- **Condition**: healthy / strained / injured, with a recovery timestamp
- **Background**: flavour + a small starting bias

### 4.3 Recruitment

Recruits appear in a **region-specific pool** that refreshes on a timer. Pool
quality scales with Presence, dojo reputation, and facilities. Recruiting costs
tuition currency. Poached students (see §8.4) enter the pool for their poacher
only, for 24 hours.

---

## 5. RESOURCES

### 5.1 Two bars, deliberately separate

- **Energy** — the PvE bar. Regenerates fast, caps low enough to overflow
  overnight. Spent on drills. This is what you burn on a mobile check-in.
- **Focus** — the PvP bar. Regenerates slowly, caps at a handful. Spent on
  scouting and challenges. Scarcity is the point: each challenge should feel
  like a decision, not a rotation.

Separating them prevents raiding from cannibalizing training, and prevents a
player from "saving up" their way past the training loop.

### 5.2 Tuition

Soft currency. Generated by facilities on timers and by branch tribute. Spent on
recruitment, facility upgrades, and accelerating branch development.

No hard currency in MVP. No purchasable Energy or Focus, ever — that trade
destroys the one thing making challenges feel weighty.

## 6. DRILLS & CURRICULA

A **drill** costs Energy, returns attribute XP to one student, and rolls on a
scroll table. Drills are assigned per-student and resolve on a timer.

### 6.1 Three-stage curricula

| Stage | Gate | Feeds | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | White belt | Strength, Stamina | None |
| **Form** | Foundation complete | Technique, Speed | Strain |
| **Application** | Form complete | All, weighted by style | Injury |

Each stage is a chain of drills, gated on the previous — the job-chain structure,
but it reads as a syllabus. Completing a curriculum promotes the student's belt.

**Repeating a completed curriculum still pays out at a reduced rate.** Critical:
Energy caps overnight, so there must always be something to spend it on, even for
a player whose whole roster is between belts.

### 6.2 Drill selection is the style input

Every drill logged adds to the school's **style vector** (see §7.2). The player
is choosing their identity every time they assign a drill, whether or not they
realize it. This is what makes drills a decision rather than a click.

---

## 7. SCROLLS & STYLE

### 7.1 Scroll sets

Drills drop **scrolls** belonging to a lineage family. Six scrolls complete a
set; completion permanently unlocks a **signature technique** teachable to any
student. Sets are the long-tail chase. Different regions drop different families,
so expansion is how you complete sets you can't finish at home.

POST-MVP: scrolls tradeable between allied dojos.

### 7.2 Style crystallization

The school maintains a running vector across the four categories (striking,
grappling, internal, weapons) plus sub-axes (aggression, patience, precision,
power). Every drill nudges it.

At defined thresholds the vector **crystallizes**: the game recognizes the shape
it has taken, the player **names their school**, and it locks as identity.

Crystallization gives you:

- a scoutable, counterable identity for PvP
- a real reason drills matter beyond XP
- an emergent community meta where lineages become known quantities
- inheritance: branches carry your style with **drift** (see §9.3)

A crystallized style can be re-forged later at significant cost, so a player who
crystallized badly in week two is not dead.

## 8. CHALLENGES

### 8.1 Structure

Costs Focus. Restricted to ±1 dojo tier. Contests **standing within a region**,
not currency.

1. **Scout** (1 Focus, or free with an informant facility) — reveals the
   defender's style composition and rough belt spread, not exact stats.
2. **Assign** — pick five students from your roster.
3. **Resolve** — automatic, instant, server-side.
4. **Report** — per-student breakdown, technique effectiveness, turning points,
   XP, injuries, standing change.

The defender is offline. Their lineup is whatever they last set as defence.

### 8.2 The style triangle

Directed triangle: **Striking → Internal → Grappling → Striking**.

**Weapons** sits off-axis: strong into everything, but fragile — low stamina
pool, punished hard by a bad matchup, and expensive to train.

Matchup advantage is significant but not deterministic. A well-trained roster
sent into a bad matchup should lose to an inferior roster maybe 65/35 — enough
that scouting matters, not so much that stats stop mattering.

### 8.3 Why this is the one real decision

Ten seconds of genuine thought. No reflexes. Fully asynchronous. It is the only
place in the game where the player is asked to solve something, and that is
deliberate — one system carrying judgment is enough, and more would break the
two-minute session.

### 8.4 Stakes: poaching, not gold

The loser's **lowest-loyalty student becomes poachable for 24 hours**. The winner
gains regional standing and first right to recruit them.

No currency changes hands. This makes losses sting in a way gold never does,
gives loyalty real weight, and creates grudges — the emotional engine of a PvP
ladder.

**Guardrails:** a dojo cannot be poached from twice in 24h. Students below a
belt threshold are not poachable. New players have a 7-day shield.

## 9. LINEAGE — THE CENTREPIECE

### 9.1 Graduation

A student who completes Application at master belt can **graduate**. They leave
the roster permanently and found a **branch dojo** in a region you choose.

Graduation is the central act of the game. It should feel like a ceremony:
named, recorded, commemorated, irreversible.

### 9.2 What branches do

- Send **tribute** (tuition + reputation) upstream, forever
- Hold **territory** in their region without you spending Focus
- Carry your **style DNA**, drifted
- **Graduate students of their own** — second generation, third, fourth
- **Defect** if neglected, taking their sub-branches with them

### 9.3 Style drift

A branch inherits the parent's style vector with a small random perturbation
plus a pull toward its region's character. By the fourth generation a branch is
recognizably descended from you but distinctly its own thing.

This is where emergent world flavour comes from at zero content cost.

### 9.4 Patronage — the tree's governor

**You have limited patronage slots** (3 at Backyard → 7 at International).

- **Patronized branch**: grows, graduates on schedule, full tribute
- **Dormant branch**: 25% tribute, no growth, slow loyalty decay, small daily
  chance to wither or defect

This is what stops the tree exploding into an unreadable spreadsheet. It also
turns lineage management into a genuine strategic layer: **breadth or depth?**

Simulation (see §12) confirms this keeps the live tree at ~5 nodes and total tree
at 28–51 nodes after a year, with a long dormant tail providing flavour and minor
income.

## 10. TIERS & GATES

Tier progression keys off **transmission**, never cash and never time alone.

| Dojo tier | Gate | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Backyard** | — | day 0 |
| **Local School** | 3 personal graduates | ~day 30 |
| **Regional Center** | a gen-1 branch reaches Local School (3 graduates of its own) | ~day 90 |
| **National Academy** | a gen-2 branch reaches Local School | ~day 180 |
| **International Institute** | style crystallized + branches in 3 regions + gen-4 exists | ~day 300 |

### 10.1 The gates alternate breadth and depth — on purpose

Simulation showed that a pure **breadth** patronage policy (always fund gen-1)
reaches Regional but stalls before National, and a pure **depth** policy (always
fund the deepest node) reaches gen-4 quickly but never satisfies the
gen-1-branch gate and stalls at Local School.

Neither pure strategy climbs the ladder. The optimal play alternates. This was an
emergent result, and it is now an intentional design requirement: **each tier gate
should demand the axis the previous one did not.**

## 11. DECAY: INJURY & LOYALTY

Two pressures that make this a management problem rather than a treadmill.

**Injury** comes from Application drills and challenge losses. Injured students
cannot be assigned. Recovery is time-based, reduced by facilities and Discipline.
Repeated injury to the same student carries a permanent attribute penalty — this
makes "rest him or push him" a real question.

**Loyalty** decays passively. It decays *faster for high-performing students* —
they know their worth. Restored by wins, shrine income, and Mental. At zero they
leave, or are poached by a rival who beat you.

The high-performer decay rule is important: it prevents the dominant strategy of
building one superstar and coasting.

---

## 12. FACILITIES

Each generates tuition on a timer and applies a passive modifier. Collected
manually; uncollected income caps at 8 hours. That cap is the return hook.

| Facility | Yield | Modifier |
|---|---|---|
| Mat space | Low, fast | +roster cap |
| Heavy bags | Medium | +Strength drill rate |
| Grappling dummies | Medium | +Technique drill rate |
| Weapons rack | Medium | +weapons drill rate |
| Sauna / ice bath | Low, slow | −injury recovery time |
| Shrine | High, slow | +loyalty |
| Informant network | None | free scouting |

## 13. THE WORLD

**Regions**, not a lobby. Each region contains:

- **Established schools** — PvE opponents, the old masters. Your difficulty curve
  and your lore. These are the future faction hooks: you are not recruited by a
  syndicate, you are *courted by a rival lineage*.
- **Player branches** — yours and everyone else's.

Regions differ in recruit quality and which scroll families drop, so expansion is
a strategic choice rather than a number going up. Standing at the top of a region
grants tribute income and first pick of recruits.

## 14. OUT OF SCOPE FOR MVP

Cut, with intent to revisit:

- Espionage and sabotage systems
- Shadow factions as joinable organizations (return as established schools)
- Separate seasonal dojo / parallel roster
- Creator economy and revenue sharing
- Voice acting
- Alliance facilities and shared venues
- Real-time or manual combat of any kind
- Player-vs-player direct messaging

Retained but deferred to post-MVP: alliances, tournaments beyond the weekly,
scroll trading, style re-forging UI.

## 15. OPEN QUESTIONS

1. **Is the drill loop too passive**, even with curriculum gating? Candidate fix:
   a small per-drill choice (intensity dial trading injury risk for XP).
2. **Is poaching too punishing in week one?** The 7-day shield may not be enough.
   Watch day-1→day-7 retention against a build with reputation-only stakes.
3. **Graduation rate is the load-bearing number.** Too fast and the tree becomes
   unreadable; too slow and second generation never arrives and the whole premise
   stays theoretical. Current model targets one personal graduate per ~10 days.
4. **Does style crystallization arrive too late to matter?** If a player hasn't
   crystallized by day 20 they may never form an identity.
5. **Defection mechanics** for dormant branches are designed but unproven — they
   may read as arbitrary punishment rather than consequence.
